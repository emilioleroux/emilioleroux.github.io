Native Frame & Shape Toolkit for Canva.

Frame & Shape Kit adds shape operations that fit naturally into Canva’s editing workflow. Combine, intersect, subtract, divide, crop and convert shapes and frames — while keeping the resulting artwork editable in Canva.
