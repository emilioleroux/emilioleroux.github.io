Geometry tools for Canva

Native geometry tools for Canva.

Frame & Shape Kit adds shape operations that fit naturally into Canva’s editing workflow. Combine, intersect, subtract, divide, crop and convert shapes and frames — while keeping the resulting artwork editable in Canva.

Convert and refine

Change compatible shapes to frames, frames to shapes, and bake rotation when you need cleaner geometry.

Built to stay in the Canva workflow.

Frame & Shape Kit creates native editable results wherever Canva’s available APIs allow it, instead of flattening your geometry into an image.
