Native geometry tools for Canva

Native Frame & Shape Toolkit for Canva.

Frame & Shape Kit adds shape operations that fit naturally into Canva’s editing workflow. Combine, intersect, subtract, divide, crop and convert shapes and frames — while keeping the resulting artwork editable in Canva.

Combine geometry

Add, intersect, subtract, XOR and divide compatible native shapes and frames directly from your Canva design.

Crop to Page

Trim geometry outside the page while keeping supported fills, borders and frame media editable.

Convert and refine

Change compatible shapes to frames, frames to shapes, and bake rotation for greater control over frame contents.

Built to stay in the Canva workflow.

Frame & Shape Kit creates native editable results wherever Canva’s available APIs allow it, instead of flattening your geometry into an image or SVG.

Unlike imported images or SVG assets, generated native shapes can retain editable fills and borders, convert between compatible shapes and frames, and be reused as the source for new geometry operations.
