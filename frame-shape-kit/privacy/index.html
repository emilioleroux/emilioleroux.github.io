Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Frame & Shape Kit is a Canva app developed by Emilio Le Roux. This policy explains the limited information used to operate the app, provide free and paid access, troubleshoot issues, and keep the service secure.

Design content

Frame & Shape Kit accesses Canva design elements and properties required to perform the tools you request, such as geometry, element type, fills, borders, transparency, rotation, and frame-related information where available through Canva.

Geometry operations are performed as part of the app’s Canva editing workflow. Frame & Shape Kit does not send your design geometry or artwork to its external backend merely to perform these geometry operations.

Account, access and usage information

To provide access to the service, Frame & Shape Kit may process and store limited account and technical information, including a Canva-provided user identifier, a Canva team or brand identifier when needed for enterprise access, daily feature-usage counts, entitlement or plan status, and timestamps associated with these records.

This information is used to determine whether you have Free, Pro, or authorized enterprise access and to enforce applicable usage limits.

Payments

If you purchase a paid plan, payment and subscription processing may be handled by a third-party payment provider. Frame & Shape Kit may store limited billing-related identifiers and subscription status needed to recognize your paid access. Payment-card details are not intended to be stored by Frame & Shape Kit itself.

Technical information

Limited technical information may be processed when necessary to operate, troubleshoot, improve, or secure the app, such as error information or technical data made available through Canva or the app’s supporting services.

How information is used

Information described above is used only to provide and maintain Frame & Shape Kit, manage access and subscriptions, apply usage limits, troubleshoot problems, prevent abuse, and improve reliability. Frame & Shape Kit does not sell personal information or use user content for advertising.

Canva and service providers

Your use of Canva remains subject to Canva’s own terms, privacy practices, and platform policies. Frame & Shape Kit may also rely on service providers for hosting, authentication, data storage, and payment processing. Those providers process information as necessary to deliver their respective services.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Frame & Shape Kit evolves. Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact frameshapekit@gmail.com.
