Support

Frame & Shape Kit is a geometry toolkit for Canva designed to extend native shape and frame workflows while keeping supported results editable inside Canva.

If you have a question, encounter an unexpected result, or find something that appears to be a bug, email frameshapekit@gmail.com.

When reporting a problem

Please include, when possible:

  • what you were trying to do;
  • which Frame & Shape Kit tool or operation you were using;
  • the types of Canva elements involved;
  • what you expected to happen and what happened instead; and
  • a screenshot if it helps explain the issue.

Please avoid sending confidential design content unless it is genuinely necessary to explain the problem.

About compatibility

Some behavior depends on the element properties Canva makes available to apps. Support can help determine whether an unexpected result is a current app limitation, a Canva platform limitation, or an issue that can be fixed.

Frame & Shape Kit is actively developed. Bug reports and practical feedback are welcome.
